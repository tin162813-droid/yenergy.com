About Us

A trusted Philippine solar energy EPC company

High-quality solar system components

Y-ENERGY GIG-ASIA INC. is a premium solar energy solutions provider specializing in the design, installation, and maintenance of solar power systems for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. We combine engineering expertise with high-quality components to deliver reliable, high-performance solar installations.

Our mission is to accelerate the Philippines' transition to clean energy by making solar power accessible, reliable, and cost-effective for every home and business we serve.
